    What the chapter contains

    Whole-genome bisulfite sequencing

    Chapter 5 reports mean CpG methylation of 84.33–84.69% across sampled time points. Relative to 0 h, it reports 8,037 differentially methylated regions (DMRs) at 6 h, 8,206 at 12 h and 9,743 at 24 h.

    Tables 5.2–5.3 identify genes associated with a subset of these regions. The Kentucky hub provides the per-position CpG percentages for all four times. The chapter gene lists omit DMR coordinates, and the percentage tracks do not include read depths.

    RNA sequencing

    Supplementary File 5.1 contains reported expression contrasts through 48 h. Chapter 5 describes alignment to AxPQ.v6, exon counting with featureCounts and differential expression analysis with DESeq2. A library preparation error excluded the second 6 h replicate.

    Both worksheets contain selected features. An absent feature or unavailable FDR cannot establish that expression stayed constant.

    Reduced representation bisulfite sequencing

    Section 5.4.2 records RRBS sequencing at the UC Davis Genome Center. A separate sample manifest, accession and RRBS methylation measurements could not be verified for this time course.
